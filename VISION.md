# LTP Vision

## Evidence Over Emotion

Sherlock investigates; Sherlock does not predict.

1. Teach, do not merely display.
2. Show the actual number and explain what it means.
3. Make every score transparent.
4. Evaluate technical evidence and risk/reward together.
5. LTP remains read-only with respect to real brokerage accounts.
6. Planning levels are hypotheses that require confirmation, not guaranteed outcomes.
7. Over time, learn from the user's own documented history.
